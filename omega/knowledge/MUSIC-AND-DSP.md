# Music / DTM / Audio DSP

Public, source-verified research index for future OMEGA Skills.

## Priority skill areas

- Synth programming, sampling, Kontakt / KSP
- JUCE / VST3 / plugin development
- MIDI sequencing and arrangement
- Vocal synthesis and voice processing
- Mixing, mastering and reproducible acoustic measurements
- Sound design, electronic music, game music and orchestration

Distinguish objective measurements from artistic judgments. Do not invent listening-test results, purchased licenses or plugin specifications.
